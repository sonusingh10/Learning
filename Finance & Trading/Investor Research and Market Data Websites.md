# Investor Research and Market Data Websites

A categorized bookmark list from the supplied roundup. Descriptions summarize the supplied text and have not been independently verified; access, features, and coverage may change. Inclusion is for research reference and is not an endorsement or investment advice.

## Investor holdings, filings, and letters

- [Dataroma](https://www.dataroma.com) — Track reported portfolios of well-known investors.
- [WhaleWisdom](https://whalewisdom.com) — Explore quarterly institutional fund filings and trades.
- [Capitol Trades](https://www.capitoltrades.com) — Track disclosed stock transactions by U.S. politicians.
- [OpenInsider](https://openinsider.com) — Search company insider stock transactions.
- [13F.info](https://13f.info) — Browse institutional 13F filings in a readable format.
- [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/edgar) — Search companies' official filings and reports.
- [Berkshire Hathaway](https://www.berkshirehathaway.com) — Find shareholder letters and company materials.
- [CNBC Buffett Archive](https://buffett.cnbc.com) — Watch archived Berkshire Hathaway annual-meeting videos.
- [Oaktree Capital memos](https://www.oaktreecapital.com/insights/memos) — Read Howard Marks's investor memos.
- [Bridgewater](https://www.bridgewater.com) — Read research and perspectives from Bridgewater Associates.
- [Principles](https://www.principles.com) — Explore Ray Dalio's principles and related learning resources.
- [GMO](https://www.gmo.com) — Read market research and investment notes from GMO.
- [AQR](https://www.aqr.com) — Explore quantitative investment research.
- [Value Investors Club](https://www.valueinvestorsclub.com) — Browse selected investment theses.

## Valuation, company research, and screening

- [Damodaran Online](https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/) — Access valuation data and teaching materials from Aswath Damodaran.
- [Musings on Markets](https://aswathdamodaran.blogspot.com) — Read Aswath Damodaran's valuation and market commentary.
- [Stock Analysis](https://stockanalysis.com) — Review company financials and market data.
- [CompaniesMarketCap](https://companiesmarketcap.com) — Compare companies by market capitalization and related rankings.
- [Finviz](https://finviz.com) — Screen stocks and explore market maps.
- [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com) — View charts and market analysis.
- [Koyfin](https://www.koyfin.com) — Explore company, market, and macroeconomic data and screening tools.
- [AnnualReports.com](https://www.annualreports.com) — Search an archive of company annual reports.
- [Morningstar](https://www.morningstar.com) — Find fund and stock research and evaluations.
- [Simply Wall St](https://simplywall.st) — Explore visual company analysis.
- [ETF Database](https://etfdb.com) — Research ETFs and their holdings.

## Economics and macro data

- [FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org) — Browse economic data maintained by the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis.
- [Federal Reserve](https://www.federalreserve.gov) — Read Federal Reserve decisions, statements, and data.
- [European Central Bank](https://www.ecb.europa.eu) — Access ECB data, publications, and policy information.
- [Bank for International Settlements](https://www.bis.org) — Read international banking and financial-system research and statistics.
- [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com) — Explore economic indicators by country.
- [Macrotrends](https://www.macrotrends.net) — Browse long-term company and market data charts.
- [Visual Capitalist](https://www.visualcapitalist.com) — Explore chart-based explainers about markets, business, and the economy.
- [Our Finite World](https://ourfiniteworld.com) — Read analysis of energy, economic, and financial-system limits.

## Portfolio construction and backtesting

- [Portfolio Visualizer](https://www.portfoliovisualizer.com) — Analyze and backtest portfolio strategies.
- [Curvo](https://curvo.eu) — Explore investment tools and historical portfolio backtests, with a European focus.
