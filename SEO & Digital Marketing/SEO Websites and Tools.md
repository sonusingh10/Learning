# SEO Websites and Tools

A categorized list of SEO, website diagnostics, research, and local-search tools from the supplied roundup. Descriptions are summarized from the post and have not been independently checked; product features, pricing, and availability can change.

## Google Search and structured data

- [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console) — Review how Google indexes and reports on a site.
- [Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search) — Official Google Search documentation.
- [Google Rich Results Test](https://search.google.com/test/rich-results) — Test structured data for rich-result eligibility.
- [Schema.org](https://schema.org) — Reference vocabulary for structured data.
- [Schema Markup Validator](https://validator.schema.org) — Validate structured data markup.
- [Google Keyword Planner](https://ads.google.com/home/tools/keyword-planner/) — Research keywords and search forecasts.
- [Google Trends](https://trends.google.com) — Explore search-interest patterns and seasonality.

## Technical SEO and site diagnostics

- [Screaming Frog SEO Spider](https://www.screamingfrog.co.uk/seo-spider/) — Crawl a site and find technical SEO issues.
- [Sitebulb](https://sitebulb.com) — Audit sites with crawl analysis and visual reports.
- [Detailed SEO Extension](https://detailed.com/extension) — Inspect on-page SEO details in a browser.
- [Redirect Checker](https://redirect-checker.org) — Inspect redirects and redirect chains.
- [httpstatus.io](https://httpstatus.io) — Check HTTP status codes for URLs in bulk.
- [Security Headers](https://securityheaders.com) — Check HTTP security headers.
- [BuiltWith](https://builtwith.com) — Identify technologies used by a website.
- [Internet Archive Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org) — View archived versions of webpages.

## Keywords, backlinks, and competitor research

- [Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com) — Research backlinks, competitors, keywords, and content opportunities.
- [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com) — SEO, paid-search, keyword, and competitor research.
- [Moz Link Explorer](https://moz.com/link-explorer) — Research backlinks and link metrics.
- [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com) — Explore estimates of website traffic and audience sources.
- [AlsoAsked](https://alsoasked.com) — Explore related People Also Ask questions.
- [Keywords Everywhere](https://keywordseverywhere.com) — Browser extension for keyword metrics and related search data.
- [Keyword Surfer](https://surferseo.com/keyword-surfer/) — Browser extension for keyword data.
- [AnswerThePublic](https://answerthepublic.com) — Discover questions and searches around a topic.

## Page speed and real-user performance

- [PageSpeed Insights](https://pagespeed.web.dev) — Check page performance and Core Web Vitals.
- [WebPageTest](https://www.webpagetest.org) — Run detailed website performance tests.
- [GTmetrix](https://gtmetrix.com) — Review page speed and performance diagnostics.
- [Chrome UX Report Dashboard](https://cruxvis.withgoogle.com) — Explore real-world Chrome user experience data.

## Local SEO and business listings

- [Local Falcon](https://localfalcon.com) — Map local search rankings across geographic grids.
- [BrightLocal](https://brightlocal.com) — Manage local SEO reporting, rankings, and citations.
- [GMB Everywhere](https://gmbeverywhere.com) — Inspect Google Business Profile categories and local competitors.
- [PlePer](https://pleper.com) — Google Business Profile category and local SEO tools.
- [Local Viking](https://localviking.com) — Google Business Profile and local ranking workflows.
- [Moz Local](https://moz.com/products/local) — Manage business listings and local presence.

## Open-source SEO repositories

- [AgriciDaniel/claude-seo](https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/claude-seo) — 24,653 stars (checked 31 Oct 2026) · MIT License. Provide a universal Claude Code SEO skill with sub-skills and sub-agents for technical, local, content, e-commerce, and international SEO workflows.
- [every-app/open-seo](https://github.com/every-app/open-seo) — 24,538 stars (checked 23 Oct 2026) · MIT License ([license](https://github.com/every-app/open-seo/blob/main/LICENSE)). Open-source alternative to Semrush and Ahrefs, with SEO research tools and agent/MCP workflows.
