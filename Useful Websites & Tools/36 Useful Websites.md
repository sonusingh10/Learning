# 36 Useful Websites

A categorized collection of useful websites gathered from two supplied lists. Descriptions, including claims about free access and paywall handling, come from those posts and have not been independently checked; availability and terms may change.

## Archives, discovery, and everyday services

1. [Wayback Machine](http://web.archive.org) — Save and revisit old webpages.
2. [Archive.ph](http://archive.ph) — Save webpages.
3. [Twelveft](http://twelveft.io) — The supplied post describes it as a way to bypass paywalls; check the publisher's access terms.
4. [Radio Garden](http://radio.garden) — Listen to radio broadcasts from around the world.
5. [FutureMe](http://futureme.org) — Send an email to your future self.
6. [Temp Mail](http://temp-mail.org) — Create a disposable email address.
7. [AlternativeTo](http://alternativeto.net) — Find alternatives to apps.
8. [BuiltWith](http://builtwith.com) — Check technologies used by websites.

## Image, PDF, and media tools

9. [Cleanup.pictures](http://cleanup.pictures) — Remove objects from images.
10. [TinyWow](http://tinywow.com) — PDF and image tools.
11. [Photopea](http://photopea.com) — Edit images in a browser.
12. [PDF24](http://pdf24.org) — PDF toolkit.
13. [Remove Photos](http://remove.photos) — Remove image backgrounds.
14. [Let's Enhance](http://letsenhance.io) — Improve image quality.
15. [Pixabay](http://pixabay.com) — Images, videos, and music.
16. [Mixkit](http://mixkit.co) — Stock videos and music.
