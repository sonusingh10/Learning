# 30 AI Websites for Creative and Productivity Workflows

Saved from the supplied list. The descriptions below reflect the post's claims, including its statements about free access, quotas, speed, and quality; those details have not been independently checked and may change.

## Image generation and editing

1. [Raphael AI](https://raphael.ai) — AI image generation; the post describes it as free and unlimited.
2. [Krea](https://krea.ai) — Real-time image generation as you draw.
3. [Magnific](https://magnific.ai) — AI image upscaling with generated detail.
4. [Clipdrop](https://clipdrop.co) — Background removal, relighting, and object erasure.
25. [remove.bg](https://www.remove.bg) — Automatic image background removal.
26. [Cleanup.pictures](https://cleanup.pictures) — Remove objects from photos.
27. [DeepSwap](https://www.deepswap.ai) — AI face-swapping.

## Voice, music, and video

5. [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io) — AI voice generation and cloning.
6. [Suno](https://suno.com) — Generate songs from prompts or lyrics.
7. [Runway](https://runwayml.com) — AI video generation.
8. [Kling AI](https://klingai.kuaishou.com) — AI video generation.
9. [D-ID](https://www.d-id.com) — Create talking-avatar videos from images and audio.
10. [SadTalker](https://sadtalker.ai) — Animate a still portrait to produce a talking video.
22. [Opus](https://opus.pro) — Find and edit short clips from longer videos.
28. [Musiio](https://musiio.com) — AI music tagging and recommendations.

## Coding and website building

11. [Cursor](https://cursor.com) — AI-assisted code editor.
12. [Bolt](https://bolt.new) — Build websites and applications from prompts.
13. [v0](https://v0.app) — Generate frontend interfaces from descriptions.
14. [Replit](https://replit.com) — Browser-based coding with AI assistance and deployment tools.

## Visuals, writing, and presentations

15. [Napkin AI](https://napkin.ai) — Turn text into diagrams and infographics.
16. [Gamma](https://gamma.app) — Create presentations from prompts or source material.
17. [Notion AI](https://www.notion.com/product/ai) — Writing, summarization, and translation within Notion.
18. [Uizard](https://uizard.io) — Turn sketches and prompts into interface designs.
29. [Jasper](https://www.jasper.ai) — AI writing and marketing assistant.

## Search, research, and meetings

19. [Perplexity](https://perplexity.ai) — AI search with cited sources.
20. [Phind](https://phind.com) — Search and assistance for software developers.
21. [Otter](https://otter.ai) — Meeting transcription and notes.
23. [Poe](https://poe.com) — Access multiple AI assistants through one service.
