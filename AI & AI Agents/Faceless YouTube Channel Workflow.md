## Turn Claude into a script engineer

The approach recommends giving Claude a structure designed for hooks, curiosity, pacing, and retention instead of asking only for “a YouTube script.”

### Sample prompt

> Write a 1,500 word YouTube script about [Topic]. Skip the corporate tone and generic intros. Open with a pattern interrupt. Drop a new curiosity loop every 45 seconds. Keep it conversational, with every section flowing into the next.

The text says Claude is useful for:

- Natural, conversational writing
- Layered story structure
- Historical and technical depth
- Long-form narrative arcs
- Research and organization

**Principle:** Better instructions lead to better output.

## Keep the production stack lean

| Job | Suggested tools |
|---|---|
| Research and script | Claude |
| Voiceover | ElevenLabs |
| Images and thumbnails | Midjourney or Flux |
| Video | Runway or Pika |
| Editing | CapCut Pro or Premiere Pro |

The recommendation is to use one tool per job and avoid an unnecessarily large tool stack.

## Three rules for scaling a faceless channel

### 1. Win the first five seconds

Skip “welcome back to the channel.” Start with conflict, a surprising statistic, an unexpected outcome, or the video's central question. The opening should earn the viewer's attention for the next ten seconds.

### 2. Batch relentlessly

Avoid making one video from start to finish each day. The suggested batch schedule is:

- **Day 1:** Draft ten scripts with Claude.
- **Day 2:** Create voiceovers and visuals.
- **Days 3–4:** Edit and schedule videos.

Batching reduces the time lost switching between tasks.

### 3. Focus on click-through rate (CTR)

A strong script needs a title and thumbnail that persuade viewers to click. Test:

- Titles
- Thumbnail concepts
- Hooks
- Visual angles
- Curiosity gaps

The text argues that even a small CTR improvement can change a video's performance.

## The real lesson

AI is described as a multiplier, not a money button. It can help with research, structure, scripting, and ideation, while the creator remains responsible for:

- Choosing the niche
- Understanding the audience
- Deciding what is worth publishing
- Improving what works

**Takeaway:** Build a repeatable workflow, respect viewers' attention, and use AI to reduce repetitive work.
